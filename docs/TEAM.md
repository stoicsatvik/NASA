# Team Recruiting Specification

Target team size: **3–4 strong people** unless additional members add a genuinely missing capability.

## Roles

### 1. Systems / product lead
Owns:
- architecture
- integration
- rapid prototyping
- prioritization
- final demo coherence

### 2. Planetary / Earth-science lead
Must be able to:
- reason about geology, geomorphology or remote sensing;
- read scientific papers rather than summarize abstracts;
- challenge invalid similarity metrics;
- explain why an Earth site is or is not a legitimate analog.

Strong signals:
- geology / geophysics / planetary science / remote sensing coursework;
- research project;
- GIS work;
- astronomy/space-science club with actual technical output.

### 3. Geospatial / data lead
Must be able to:
- work with raster/vector data;
- use Python;
- handle coordinate systems;
- process satellite products;
- reason about uncertainty and missing data.

Strong signals:
- rasterio / GDAL / xarray / GeoPandas / QGIS;
- remote-sensing or climate project;
- reproducible notebooks;
- evidence of independently shipping.

### 4. Visualization / story lead
Must be able to turn a scientific result into:
- an intuitive map;
- side-by-side comparisons;
- a 60-second judge demo;
- clean narrative/presentation.

Design talent without scientific curiosity is insufficient.

## Recruiting test

Do not ask candidates "Do you know AI?"

Ask one of these:

1. Pick an Earth site and explain what measurements would make it a credible Mars analog.
2. Given two raster datasets at different resolutions, how would you compare them without lying to yourself?
3. Show one project where you personally owned a hard technical or scientific decision.
4. What result would make you abandon your current hypothesis?
5. Explain one dataset you have actually worked with.

## Candidate score

Each candidate: 0–5 on

- independent execution
- complementary expertise
- evidence of past work
- scientific rigor
- communication
- reliability
- curiosity

A candidate with a lower raw skill level but high independence/reliability may be more valuable in a 48-hour event than a nominal expert who disappears.

## Avoid

- six generalist web developers;
- people joining only for the certificate;
- "AI expert" with no measurable project;
- teammate who cannot explain their own GitHub;
- domain person unwilling to challenge the product;
- prestige recruiting for its own sake.
